# HAVONE Image Generation Prompts

Use these as final-ready prompts for GPT Image models. Keep the visual language consistent: enterprise autonomous mobility, precise hardware, restrained corporate polish, deep black and white spaces, signal green accents, documentary realism, no cartoon look, no exaggerated sci-fi.

## Global Negative Direction

Avoid: toy-like vehicles, fantasy cities, unreadable UI text, overdone neon, purple gradients, orange/brown palettes, generic stock photo mood, distorted wheels, incorrect road geometry, visible brand logos from other companies.

## 1. Homepage Hero

Prompt:

```text
A cinematic editorial hero image for HAVONE MOBILITY, an enterprise autonomous mobility company. A clean autonomous electric shuttle-truck platform moves through a modern logistics campus at dawn, viewed from a low three-quarter front angle. Subtle roof lidar, side radar modules, camera pods, and signal green status light accents are visible. The environment is premium and realistic: glass operations center, clean loading bays, lane markings, light mist, quiet corporate atmosphere. Leave usable dark negative space on the left side for website headline text. Color palette: deep black, graphite, cool white, signal green accents. Photorealistic, high-end commercial automotive photography, 35mm lens, crisp reflections, natural lighting, no text, no logos, no people in foreground.
```

Recommended: `1536x1024`, high quality.
